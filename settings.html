Password

Change your password with a one-time link sent to your own e-mail address. The link opens a page on the hub where you choose the new password; it works once and for 24 hours. Every other sign-in of your account ends when the new password goes live.

People

Who can sign in to the hub. Passwords are never stored: each account keeps only a salted fingerprint. Changes made here are queued below and go live when you publish them.

NameUser IDRoleDivisions

Change a role with the drop-down, choose which divisions a viewer may open under Divisions (admins and super admins see all), or use Reset password (the person receives a one-time link to choose a new one) and Remove. Changes are queued below and go out as one e-mail to the hub mailbox; the hub applies them within about ten minutes and tells the people concerned.

Access and reset requests

Checking the mailbox list…

    Requests reach the mailbox mml@bpropms.in and are listed above within about five minutes. Approve by e-mail opens a one-word reply; send it from the hub mailbox (or from a super admin's address, keeping mml@bpropms.in as the recipient) and the hub creates the account and e-mails the person their user ID and temporary password, with no token needed. Password resets are handled automatically with a one-time link to the person's own address. If a request is not listed yet, paste the e-mail (or just its code starting MGQ1.) here.
