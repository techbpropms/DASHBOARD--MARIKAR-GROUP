Forgot your password? Four steps, about two minutes

  1. Open mmlpbi.bpropms.in and press Forgot password?
  2. Enter your user ID and press E-mail me a code. The e-mail "Marikar hub: choose a new password" arrives within a minute (look in Spam or Junk too).
  3. Type the 8-character code from the e-mail (like K7QM-3XPA) and your new password twice, then press Set my password.
  4. When the page says "Your password is live", press Sign in. Keep the 30-day box ticked, and you are in.
