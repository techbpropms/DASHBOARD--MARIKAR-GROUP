Two corrections were needed, because the export drops minus signs

121 service bills with a negative total

On these the discount exceeded the bill. Tally's Grand Total treats them as negative, but the rows show them as positive. Taken at face value they overstate gross by ₹14,057.44 (2 × ₹7,028.72). We detect them from each bill's ledger arithmetic and flip their sign.

2Round-off amounts

As shown they add up to ₹1,864.77, against a net of ₹27.11 in the Grand Total, because negative round-offs lose their sign. The effect on revenue is immaterial, but any model must not add this column at face value.

IGST is not a column here because all sales are intra-state. The GSTIN column is filled on only 75 bills (B2B customers).

06 | Export notes for the other companies

The pilot shows what to ask for up front, so later exports need no rework.

1Keep the pilot's layout

Use the register's item-wise view (bill row, then item rows) with every ledger as its own column. That is what lets totals tie column by column.

2Keep minus signs

If Tally's Excel export drops signs, also send the register as XML or with separate debit and credit columns, so negative bills and round-offs keep their sign.

3Save as a real .xls or .xlsx

Avoid exporting an .xlsx under an .xls name.

4Separate customer from vehicle

"Particulars" combines the customer's name with the vehicle number, and some phone numbers too. The dashboard needs neither, so export the party ledger cleanly or masked and share the files with fewer people.

5Include the Grand Total row

It is the reconciliation anchor for check 02.

6One period per file, whole months

Name each file by company code and period exactly as in playbook section 05.
