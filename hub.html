Operating profit is at a four-year high (₹5.01 Cr). The fall in net profit comes from the cost of debt, not from trading.

Borrowings have more than doubled in two years and now match net worth. In FY 2025–26, operating cash covered under half of the year's debt service (0.46x, against at least 1x for comfort).

Ask Accounts: how much of the new borrowing funds stock, and is moving the new term loans to longer tenors planned? A coverage ratio below 1x needs an explanation before any lender or banker reviews the accounts.
