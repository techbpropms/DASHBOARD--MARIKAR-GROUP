Signing in, a forgotten password, and setting a new one

Five short steps, all on the sign-in page. Nobody needs to approve anything: the code sent to your own mailbox is the proof that the account is yours.

Before you start: load the latest version once. On a computer press Ctrl + F5 (Mac: Cmd + Shift + R) on the sign-in page. On a phone, close the hub app or tab completely and open mmlpbi.bpropms.in again. Only needed once.

A. Signing in

  1. Open mmlpbi.bpropms.in.
  2. Type your user ID: your official e-mail address, spelt exactly (for example name@marikarengineers.com). If it is misspelt, the page now says so.
  3. Type your password. Capital and small letters count; press Show to check what you typed. Then press Sign in.

B. Forgot your password, or never set one

  1. On the sign-in page press Forgot password?
  2. Enter your user ID and press E-mail me a code. Within seconds an e-mail "Marikar hub: choose a new password" arrives in that mailbox (look in Spam or Junk if it is not in the inbox).
  3. Take the code from that e-mail: eight letters and digits, like K7QM-3XPA. Always use the newest e-mail: every new code cancels the earlier ones.
  4. Back on the sign-in page, type the code, then your new password twice: at least 10 characters, with letters and at least one number or symbol, and not your user ID. Press Set my password.
  5. Wait a few seconds for "Your password is live", press Sign in, and sign in with the new password. A confirmation e-mail follows.

The e-mail also has a link: opening it lets you choose the password on a separate page instead. Either way works; use one, not both.
